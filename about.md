## Why CheapFly exists

Flying from Canada is expensive. Long distances, a small number of large airlines, high airport fees and a population spread across a huge country all push fares up. Yet every week, somewhere, a fare drops far below normal — a sale, a new route, a fuel-price swing or an outright pricing mistake. Most travellers never see those moments in time.

CheapFly.ca exists to close that gap. We watch fares from 16 Canadian airports, publish clear deal thresholds so you can recognise a great price on your own, and send free alerts when fares cross them.

## What we do

- **Free deal alerts** personalised by home airport, region, travel months and budget. [Set yours up]({{ '/deal-alerts/' | relative_url }}).
- **A price watch** with deal thresholds for 80+ routes, so you know what "cheap" really means on your route.
- **Guides** on booking windows, mistake fares, budget airlines, points, passenger rights and more.
- **Airport and destination pages** with local, practical advice for each city.
- **Free tools** — from a [delay compensation calculator]({{ '/tools/flight-delay-compensation/' | relative_url }}) to a [carry-on size checker]({{ '/tools/carry-on-checker/' | relative_url }}).

## What we don't do

We are not a travel agency and we do not sell tickets. We never charge booking fees or mark up fares. When you find a flight through CheapFly, you book directly with the airline or the booking site you choose.

## How we stay independent

CheapFly is funded by display advertising (Google AdSense), clearly labelled sponsorships and voluntary [reader support]({{ '/support/' | relative_url }}). Advertisers and sponsors never influence our deal thresholds, recommendations or editorial content. Read our [editorial policy]({{ '/editorial-policy/' | relative_url }}) for details on how we research, fact-check and correct our work.

## Who we are

CheapFly.ca is written and maintained by the **CheapFly Travel Desk**, an independent editorial team focused on Canadian air travel. We check our guides against official sources, test every tool we build, and answer reader questions personally.

We're always looking for contributors — deal analysts, writers, video creators and ambassadors. See [open roles]({{ '/careers/' | relative_url }}).
