How to use the multi-engine search

Type a city or airport code in From — for example YYZ, Calgary or Halifax — and pick a destination, or leave To empty to explore. Choose your dates, travellers and cabin, select an engine and press Search flights. The results open in a new tab, and links appear underneath so you can run the identical search on every other engine without retyping.

Five habits that find cheaper fares

  1. Check nearby airports. Toronto travellers should compare YYZ, YTZ and YHM; Vancouver travellers should look at YXX and Bellingham (BLI). Our airport guides list the alternatives for every major city.
  2. Move your dates by a day or two. Tuesday, Wednesday and Saturday departures are often cheaper. Use the date grid on Google Flights to see the whole week at once.
  3. Price one-ways separately. On some transborder and sun routes, two one-way tickets on different airlines beat a return fare.
  4. Compare the true cost. Basic fares may exclude a carry-on bag and seat selection. Run your bag through the carry-on checker before comparing prices.
  5. Know a good price when you see one. Our price watch lists deal thresholds for 80+ routes from Canada.

Engines show prices in Canadian dollars by default from Canada, but taxes and bag fees vary by site. Always confirm the final total on the booking page before you pay.
