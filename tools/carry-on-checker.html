How to avoid bag fees on Canadian flights

Bag fees are where cheap fares stop being cheap. Checked bags commonly cost a meaningful amount each way on North American and sun routes, and a carry-on may not be included on the lowest fare. Travelling with a bag that fits the personal item limit is the single most reliable way to fly any airline at the advertised price.

  • Measure the packed bag, including wheels and handles.
  • Choose soft-sided bags for personal items — they compress to fit sizers.
  • Prepay online if you need a bag; gate fees are usually higher.
  • Wear your bulkiest items on travel day.

Airline limits change often and can differ by route and fare. This checker uses published limits we reviewed recently, but always confirm on your airline's website before you fly.
