Codes Canadian deal hunters should know

Knowing codes speeds up every search, and city codes unlock cheaper combinations. Search YTO to include Toronto Pearson (YYZ), Billy Bishop (YTZ) and sometimes Hamilton (YHM) in one go, or YMQ for Montréal. Border-adjacent travellers should also memorise BUF (Buffalo), BLI (Bellingham), PBG (Plattsburgh) and BTV (Burlington) — they often undercut Canadian airports on US and sun routes.
