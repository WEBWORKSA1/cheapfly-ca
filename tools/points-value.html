Your redemption

How to judge a points redemption

The formula is simple: (cash price − taxes and fees on the award) ÷ points required × 100 = cents per point. The hard part is knowing what number is good. Points are a currency whose value depends entirely on how you spend them. The same balance can be worth 1 cent per point on a short domestic hop and 3 cents or more on a long-haul premium-cabin seat.

Rules of thumb

  • Redeem when value is high and cash prices are high — peak dates, last-minute trips and premium cabins.
  • Pay cash when fares are on sale — a cheap sale fare makes points look poor value, and you earn points on the ticket.
  • Watch surcharges — some partner airlines add large fees to award tickets that destroy the value.
