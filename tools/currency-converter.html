Spend smarter abroad

Exchange-rate fees are a hidden cost on almost every international trip. Most Canadian credit cards add a foreign-transaction fee of about 2.5% on purchases in other currencies, and airport exchange counters often charge far more through poor rates. On a two-week trip, those markups can cost as much as a night in a hotel.

  • Always pay in local currency when a card machine asks — dynamic currency conversion usually costs more.
  • Use a no-FX-fee card for purchases. Several Canadian cards waive the fee; see our travel credit cards guide.
  • Withdraw larger amounts less often to limit per-withdrawal ATM fees.
  • Carry two cards on different payment networks in case one is declined or blocked.
