Your trip costs (CAD)

Build a budget that survives the trip

The flight is usually the single biggest cost, but it is rarely more than a third of the total. Accommodation, food and the small daily costs add up faster than most people expect — especially on longer trips. Planning every line in advance lets you see where a cheaper choice makes the biggest difference.

Where Canadians typically save the most

  • Flights: flexible dates and alerts often cut fares by a quarter or more. Set a target with our price watch.
  • Bags: travelling carry-on only avoids checked-bag fees on both legs. Check your bag with the carry-on checker.
  • Exchange fees: a no-foreign-transaction-fee card avoids the typical 2.5% markup on every purchase abroad.
  • Accommodation: a kitchen can halve food costs on longer stays.

The monthly savings figure divides your total by the months until departure. Automating that transfer the day after payday is the simplest way to arrive debt-free. For more ideas, see how to find cheap flights from Canada.
