Why distance matters when you hunt for deals

Fares are not priced by distance, which is exactly why distance is useful. When a 7,000 km flight to Europe costs less than a 1,500 km domestic hop, you know you are looking at a genuinely good deal. Comparing price per kilometre across options helps you spot value quickly.

Distance also drives comfort decisions. Above roughly 6–7 hours, an aisle seat, a bit of extra legroom or a stopover can be worth paying for. Below that, the cheapest basic fare is often fine if your bag fits the rules.
