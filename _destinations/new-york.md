New York is one of the easiest cheap city breaks from central Canada, with dozens of daily flights from Toronto, Montréal and Ottawa and a choice of three major airports. Fares between Canada and New York are often lowest in January and February, while hotels are the bigger budget swing. Search all three airports at once and fly midweek to find the best price.

## Quick answer

- **Three airports:** JFK (international hub), LaGuardia (LGA, closest to Manhattan) and Newark (EWR, in New Jersey, often cheapest).
- **Best gateways:** Toronto Pearson, Billy Bishop, Montréal and Ottawa, with nonstops from Vancouver, Calgary and Halifax.
- **Cheapest months:** January–February and late August; autumn and December are priciest.
- **Deal threshold:** roughly $200–$300 return from Toronto, Montréal or Ottawa, taxes included.
- **Big saver:** take airport transit instead of a taxi, and stay outside Midtown.

## Which Canadian airports fly to New York

Competition on Canada–New York routes is intense. Air Canada links Toronto, Montréal, Ottawa, Vancouver and other cities with LaGuardia, Newark and JFK. Porter flies to Newark from [Billy Bishop]({{ '/airports/ytz/' | relative_url }}), Ottawa and Montréal, and added LaGuardia service in 2025. WestJet also serves the New York area on selected routes, and US airlines including Delta, American and United fly from several Canadian cities into their New York hubs.

From [Toronto Pearson]({{ '/airports/yyz/' | relative_url }}), Montréal or Ottawa, you clear US customs before departure through preclearance, so you land as a domestic passenger. Schedules shift with demand, and Canadian airlines trimmed some US flying in 2026, so confirm your route is operating (verify before you fly).

> **Heads up:** Billy Bishop has no US preclearance. Porter passengers from the island airport clear US customs on arrival at Newark, which can add time at busy periods.

## Flight-only vs package

New York is almost always a flight-only trip. Flight-and-hotel bundles from online travel agencies can occasionally save money in the busy autumn season, but compare against booking separately, and check whether the hotel adds a daily "destination" or facility fee.

## Money-saving tips for New York

- **Stay outside Midtown.** Long Island City, parts of Brooklyn and Jersey City offer cheaper rooms with quick subway or PATH train access to Manhattan.
- **Tap to pay.** The subway and buses accept contactless cards and phones, with a weekly fare cap once you reach a set number of rides on the same card.
- **Enjoy free sights.** The Staten Island Ferry, Central Park, the High Line and many gallery districts cost nothing.
- **Discount theatre tickets.** Same-day discount booths sell reduced-price Broadway and off-Broadway seats.
- **Skip taxis into Manhattan.** Congestion tolls, surcharges and tips make car rides from the airports expensive.

## Entry requirements in brief

Canadians need a valid passport or NEXUS card to fly to the US. Since late December 2025, US officers photograph non-citizens, including Canadians, on entry and exit. Stays of 30 days or longer carry a US registration requirement.

## Getting from the airport

- **JFK:** the AirTrain connects terminals with the subway and with commuter rail at Jamaica station. Expect a separate AirTrain fee plus your subway or rail fare.
- **LaGuardia:** a free airport bus runs to subway stations in Queens; regular city buses also run to upper Manhattan.
- **Newark:** the AirTrain links to the airport rail station, where commuter trains reach Penn Station in Manhattan in roughly 30 minutes.
- **Taxis:** JFK has a flat fare to Manhattan before surcharges, tolls and tip; other trips are metered.

## Bottom line

New York is a short, competitive flight from central Canada, and the cheapest trips happen in January, February and late August.
