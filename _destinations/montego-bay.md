Montego Bay is Jamaica's tourism gateway and one of the best-connected Caribbean airports for Canadians, especially from Ontario. Its airport sits minutes from the Hip Strip and the resort corridor, so you can be on the beach within an hour of landing. For the lowest fares, target May–June or the autumn shoulder and book well away from Christmas and March break.

## Quick answer

- **Best gateways:** Toronto year-round, plus seasonal winter nonstops from Montréal, Ottawa, Halifax and Calgary.
- **New for winter 2026–27:** Porter announced Montego Bay flights from Toronto Pearson, Ottawa and Hamilton (subject to regulatory approvals at announcement time).
- **Cheapest months:** May–June and September–October.
- **Deal threshold:** roughly $400–$500 return from Toronto, taxes included.
- **Recovery check:** Hurricane Melissa hit western Jamaica in October 2025, so confirm your resort is fully open before you book.

## Which Canadian airports fly to Montego Bay

Montego Bay's airport (MBJ) gets most of its Canadian service from Air Canada, WestJet (which took over Sunwing's airline flying in 2025) and Air Transat. [Toronto Pearson]({{ '/airports/yyz/' | relative_url }}) has the deepest schedule, while Montréal, Ottawa, Halifax and Calgary typically see seasonal winter nonstops.

Porter's winter 2026–27 plan adds competition: Toronto Pearson from late November, Ottawa from late November and [Hamilton]({{ '/airports/yhm/' | relative_url }}) from December 20, 2026, at a few flights a week. Those routes were announced as subject to regulatory approvals, so check that they are actually on sale (verify before you fly). From Vancouver, Edmonton and Winnipeg, expect one stop via Toronto, Calgary or a US hub.

## A note on Hurricane Melissa

Hurricane Melissa struck western Jamaica in late October 2025, and the Montego Bay area was among the hardest hit. The airport resumed commercial flights within days, many resorts reopened during the 2025–26 winter, and Canada later returned Jamaica's travel advisory to its pre-storm level. Some larger properties needed longer repairs and reopened on later dates.

> **Heads up:** Before you pay, confirm that your specific resort is fully open, with all restaurants and pools operating. Some recovering properties run at reduced capacity, which can show up as unusually cheap packages.

## Flight-only vs package

Montego Bay works both ways. Packages are excellent value for the big all-inclusive strips at Rose Hall and along the coast toward Negril, and they include the airport transfer. Flight-only makes sense if you want a guesthouse on the Hip Strip, a villa, or a split stay between Negril and Ocho Rios. > **Pro tip:** Negril resorts often price lower than comparable Montego Bay properties, and many packages include the roughly 90-minute transfer at no extra charge.

## Money-saving tips for Montego Bay

- **Use Jamaican dollars locally.** US cash works at resorts, but change usually comes back in Jamaican dollars at a poor rate.
- **Withdraw from bank ATMs** rather than standalone machines, and decline dynamic currency conversion.
- **Eat where locals eat.** Jerk centres and cook shops cost a fraction of resort à-la-carte add-ons.
- **Book excursions directly** with licensed operators; resort desks often add a markup.
- **Insure autumn trips.** See our [travel insurance guide]({{ '/guides/travel-insurance-for-canadians/' | relative_url }}). This is general information, not professional advice.

## Getting from the airport

- **Package transfers:** included in most packages.
- **Licensed taxis:** official airport taxis use posted route rates; confirm the price and currency before leaving.
- **Hip Strip hotels:** about 10 minutes from the terminal by road.
- **Intercity coaches:** scheduled coaches link Montego Bay with Ocho Rios, Negril and Kingston for much less than a private transfer.
- **Route taxis:** the cheapest local option, but crowded and impractical with luggage.

## Bottom line

Montego Bay is an easy, well-served Caribbean escape from Ontario and Québec, and new Porter flying should sharpen winter fares. Target spring and late autumn, confirm your resort's post-hurricane status, and insure the trip.
