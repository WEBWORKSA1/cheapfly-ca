Honolulu is Canada's most accessible Hawaiian gateway, with nonstop flights from Vancouver and seasonal service from Calgary and Toronto. Fares are highest when Canadians most want to escape winter, so the best prices come in late spring and early autumn. Book with flexible dates, and budget for Hawaii's higher 2026 hotel taxes.

## Quick answer

- **Best gateway:** Vancouver, with nonstops from Air Canada and WestJet; Calgary and Toronto have seasonal or less frequent service.
- **Cheapest months:** late April to May and September to early November.
- **Deal threshold:** roughly $550–$700 return from Vancouver, taxes included.
- **New cost:** Hawaii's state hotel tax rose to 11% on January 1, 2026, before county and excise taxes.
- **Transfer tip:** buses to Waikiki are cheap, but bring only luggage you can hold on your lap.

## Which Canadian airports fly to Honolulu

[Vancouver]({{ '/airports/yvr/' | relative_url }}) has the strongest Hawaii schedule, with Air Canada and WestJet flying to Honolulu (HNL) and to Maui. [Calgary]({{ '/airports/yyc/' | relative_url }}) has WestJet service, and Air Canada has operated seasonal Toronto–Honolulu flights in winter. Edmonton has had seasonal flights in some years.

Reports suggest WestJet trimmed some Canada–Hawaii capacity for winter 2026–27, so confirm that your route runs on your dates (verify before you fly). From other cities, you'll usually connect in Vancouver, Calgary, Toronto or a US west coast hub such as Seattle, San Francisco or Los Angeles.

## Flight-only vs package

Hawaii packages from Canadian airlines' vacation arms can be good value in Waikiki, where large hotels sell rooms in bulk, and they sometimes include transfers. Flight-only is better if you plan to island-hop, stay in a condo, or use hotel points. Always compare the package price with the flight plus the hotel's full nightly cost, including resort fees and taxes.

## Money-saving tips for Honolulu

- **Budget for taxes and fees.** Hawaii's state transient accommodations tax rose from 10.25% to 11% on January 1, 2026; county taxes and general excise tax come on top, and many hotels add daily resort fees.
- **Skip the rental car in Waikiki.** Hotel parking is expensive. Rent for a day or two for the North Shore and windward coast instead.
- **Use the transit card.** Honolulu's reloadable fare card works on buses and the Skyline rail line, with daily fare caps.
- **Reserve popular sites early.** Diamond Head and Hanauma Bay use advance reservations with fees for non-residents.
- **Eat like a local.** Plate lunches, poke counters and grocery-store prepared foods cost far less than resort restaurants.

## Entry requirements in brief

Canadians need a valid passport or NEXUS card to fly to the US. Since late December 2025, US officers photograph non-citizens, including Canadians, on entry and exit. US preclearance in Vancouver, Calgary and Toronto means you arrive in Honolulu as a domestic passenger. Hawaii also requires an agricultural declaration for arriving passengers, because many plants, fruits and animals are restricted.

## Getting from the airport

- **Public bus:** routes run from the airport to Waikiki for a low flat fare, but luggage must fit on your lap or under the seat.
- **Skyline rail:** a station opened at the airport in October 2025, but the line does not reach Waikiki yet; it is most useful for Pearl Harbor and points west.
- **Shared shuttles:** affordable for solo travellers and couples with suitcases.
- **Rideshare and taxis:** about 20 to 45 minutes to Waikiki depending on traffic.

> **Heads up:** Afternoon traffic between the airport and Waikiki can double the ride time. Allow extra time on your departure day.
