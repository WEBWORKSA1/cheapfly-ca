Paris is one of the best-served European cities from Canada, thanks to Montréal's deep links with France and strong competition between Air Canada, Air France and Air Transat. The lowest fares typically appear in late winter and November, while late spring and early autumn offer the best balance of price and weather. Two border changes matter in 2026: the EU's new biometric Entry/Exit System is live, and ETIAS is still not.

## Quick answer

- **Best gateways:** Montréal and Toronto year-round, plus Vancouver, Québec City, Calgary and Halifax with seasonal or less frequent nonstops.
- **Cheapest months:** January–March (outside school breaks) and November.
- **Deal threshold:** roughly $550–$700 return from Montréal or Toronto, taxes included.
- **Border:** visa-free for up to 90 days in any 180; expect biometric registration on your first entry.
- **ETIAS:** not launched as of November 2026 and no start date confirmed (verify before you fly).

## Which Canadian airports fly to Paris

[Montréal]({{ '/airports/yul/' | relative_url }}) is Canada's main gateway to Paris–Charles de Gaulle (CDG), with multiple daily flights from Air Canada, Air France and Air Transat in peak season. Toronto has the same three airlines. Air France says it will run up to 34 weekly flights from Canada to CDG in winter 2026–27, rising to 45 from mid-December.

Vancouver has nonstop service from Air Canada and Air France, and [Québec City]({{ '/airports/yqb/' | relative_url }}) typically gets seasonal flights. WestJet keeps Paris in its summer Halifax schedule, and Calgary has seen seasonal summer nonstops. Edmonton, Winnipeg and Ottawa travellers usually connect through Montréal or Toronto.

## Entry rules: EES and ETIAS

The EU's Entry/Exit System (EES) began rolling out in October 2025 and became fully operational across the Schengen Area on April 10, 2026. It replaces passport stamps with a digital record of your fingerprints and a facial photo, captured on your first entry. Allow extra time at the border, especially on that first trip.

ETIAS, the online travel authorisation for visa-exempt visitors such as Canadians, is a separate system. As of late October 2026, the EU had dropped its late-2026 target and announced no start date, with reporting pointing to 2027. A transition period of at least six months is planned after launch.

> **Heads up:** Any website charging for ETIAS today is not official. When it launches, apply only through the official EU channels. Check travel.gc.ca before every trip.

## Flight-only vs package

Paris is overwhelmingly a flight-only destination. Hotel bundles can save money in summer and around big events, but compare them against booking separately and check the neighbourhood and nearest metro line.

## Money-saving tips for Paris

- **Use a weekly pass if you are staying several days.** The Navigo weekly pass runs Monday to Sunday and covers zones that include both airports.
- **Eat the set lunch.** Many restaurants offer a fixed-price lunch menu that costs far less than dinner.
- **Picnic and use bakeries.** A bakery lunch in a park is a classic budget meal.
- **Stay a little outside the centre.** Neighbourhoods near a direct metro line in the outer arrondissements are cheaper than the historic core.
- **Pay in euros** and decline dynamic currency conversion on card terminals.

## Getting from the airport

- **RER B train:** the main budget route to Gare du Nord, Châtelet and the Left Bank. A dedicated airport ticket cost €14 in 2026; regular metro tickets are not valid for the airport trip.
- **Bus alternatives:** the Roissybus to Opéra was discontinued in March 2026, so the RER B is now the standard public option; slower local buses connect to the metro network.
- **Taxis:** official taxis charge fixed fares to central Paris, roughly €56 to the Right Bank and €65 to the Left Bank at last check.
- **Rideshare:** prices vary with demand and traffic.

## Bottom line

Paris is easiest and cheapest from Montréal and Toronto, especially in late winter and November. Allow extra time for the EU's new biometric border checks, ignore anyone selling ETIAS before it exists, and take the RER B into the city.
