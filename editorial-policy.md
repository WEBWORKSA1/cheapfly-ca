Our readers make real decisions — and spend real money — based on what we publish. These standards explain how we earn and keep that trust.

## Accuracy first

- We rely on **primary sources**: airline websites and tariffs, airport authorities, the Canadian Transportation Agency, travel.gc.ca, CATSA and other official bodies.
- We **verify deals** before alerting subscribers: dates must be bookable, prices must include taxes, and bag and seat rules are noted.
- Changeable details — fees, baggage sizes, entry rules, passenger-rights amounts — are dated and hedged, and we tell readers to confirm before they travel.
- Deal thresholds are reviewed regularly against fare patterns and adjusted when markets change.

## How we write

Our guides are written for Canadian travellers in plain language, with Canadian spelling and prices in Canadian dollars. We use research and writing tools, including AI-assisted drafting, to help us work efficiently; every page is checked against sources and edited by the CheapFly Travel Desk before publication. We never publish fabricated statistics, quotes, testimonials or reviews.

## Independence

Advertisers, sponsors and partners never pay for favourable coverage, never change a deal threshold and never see editorial content before publication unless it is a clearly labelled sponsored piece. Ads served by Google AdSense are chosen by Google's systems, not by our editors.

## Labelling

- **Advertisement** — display ads.
- **Sponsored / Presented by** — paid placements and co-created content.
- **Affiliate links** — disclosed in our [disclaimer]({{ '/disclaimer/' | relative_url }}).

## Updates and corrections

We review high-traffic guides at least every six months and whenever rules change. Each guide shows its last-updated date. If you spot an error, please [tell us]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}). We correct factual errors promptly and note significant corrections on the page.

## Reader submissions

Deal tips, videos and contest entries from readers are verified before publication, credited with permission, and published only if they meet these standards.
