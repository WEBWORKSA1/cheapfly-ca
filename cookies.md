This Cookie Policy explains how CheapFly.ca uses cookies and similar technologies. Read it together with our [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}).

## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites place on your device. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels and scripts. They help sites work, remember your preferences and measure performance, and they allow advertising partners to show relevant ads.

## What we use

| Type | Set by | Purpose | Can you refuse? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Essential preferences (local storage) | CheapFly.ca | Remembers dark mode, your cookie choice, dismissed pop-ups and subscription status | Clear your browser storage at any time |
| Advertising cookies | Google AdSense and its partners | Show and measure ads, limit repetition, and personalise ads based on your browsing | Yes — choose "Essential only", or use the opt-out links below |
| Analytics (if enabled) | Google Analytics | Counts visits and popular pages in aggregate | Yes — use browser settings or Google's opt-out add-on |
| Embedded video | YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode) | Plays videos only after you press play | Yes — don't play embedded videos |
| Form processing | FormSubmit | Delivers form submissions; does not set tracking cookies on this site | Not applicable |

## Your choices

- **Our cookie notice:** choose "Accept all" or "Essential only". "Essential only" asks Google to serve non-personalised ads.
- **Google:** manage ad personalisation at [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).
- **Industry opt-outs:** [youradchoices.ca](https://youradchoices.ca/) (Canada) and [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).
- **Browser controls:** every major browser lets you block or delete cookies. Blocking all cookies may affect how the Site works.

Visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland see a consent message from a Google-certified consent management platform where required.

We may update this policy when our tools or partners change. Check the date above for the latest version.
