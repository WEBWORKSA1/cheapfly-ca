Official rules (summary)

Each contest round is governed by specific rules published on this page when the round opens. The following standard terms apply to every CheapFly.ca contest unless the specific rules say otherwise.

  1. Sponsor: CheapFly.ca, together with any co-sponsor named in the round's rules.
  2. Eligibility: legal residents of Canada, excluding Quebec, who have reached the age of majority in their province or territory. Employees of the sponsor and their households are not eligible.
  3. No purchase necessary. Payment or contribution never increases your chances.
  4. Skill: entries are judged on published criteria. Selected entrants must correctly answer a mathematical skill-testing question, without assistance, within a time limit before being declared a winner.
  5. Entry period, prizes and approximate retail value are stated for each round. Prizes must be accepted as awarded and are not transferable unless stated.
  6. Original work: entries must be your own; you grant CheapFly.ca a non-exclusive licence to display them with credit.
  7. Privacy: personal information is used only to run the contest and as described in our privacy policy.
  8. Release: winners may be asked to sign a declaration and release before receiving a prize.

This summary does not replace the full rules for a specific round. Contests are void where prohibited by law.
