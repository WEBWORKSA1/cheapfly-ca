Saskatoon's best flight deals are WestJet winter sun nonstops and domestic sales, with Air Canada and Porter keeping fares honest on key eastern routes. The cheapest fares usually appear from mid-January to February, in late spring and in the fall, and it often pays to compare Regina too.

## Quick answer

- **Main carriers:** WestJet has the most routes, with Air Canada, Porter and Flair competing on some.
- **Cheapest months:** mid-January to February, late April to May, and September to November.
- **Best-value routes:** Cancún, Las Vegas, Phoenix, Calgary and Toronto.
- **Cheap airport access:** Saskatoon Transit Route 11.
- **Alternatives:** Regina for different sun routes, Edmonton or Calgary for long-haul trips.

## Airlines and where they fly

WestJet is Saskatoon's biggest airline by far, with nonstop flights to Calgary, Edmonton, Vancouver, Toronto and other Canadian cities, plus a busy winter sun program. It has also flown seasonally between Saskatoon and Halifax, a handy connection for Atlantic Canada and WestJet's summer Europe network.

Air Canada links YXE with its hubs, including Toronto, Calgary and Vancouver. Porter has added Saskatoon to its jet network, and Flair has offered low-cost domestic routes at times. Rise Air serves northern Saskatchewan, and US carriers have offered limited service to their hubs.

> **Heads up:** With fewer airlines than big-city airports, Saskatoon fares can jump when a competitor leaves a route. When you see a good fare on a route with only one carrier, book it rather than waiting.

## Cheapest times to fly from Saskatoon

Like the rest of the Prairies, Saskatoon's calendar revolves around winter sun. Prices spike over Christmas, the February long weekend and spring break, then ease in late January and again from April.

| Period | What to expect from YXE |
|---|---|
| Mid-January to February | Good sun fares outside the long weekend |
| March to early April | Spring-break peaks |
| Late April to May | Shoulder value to the US and Canada |
| July–August | Peak domestic pricing |
| September to November | Strong fall value and early sun sales |

## Best-value routes from YXE

Cancún is Saskatoon's most dependable winter deal; see our [Cancún guide]({{ '/destinations/cancun/' | relative_url }}) for what a good fare looks like. Las Vegas and Phoenix are popular shorter escapes, and Punta Cana and Puerto Vallarta appear seasonally.

Within Canada, Calgary, Edmonton and Vancouver go on sale regularly, and Toronto can be good value when Air Canada, WestJet and Porter overlap.

> **Pro tip:** All-inclusive packages from Saskatoon can be cheaper than buying a flight and hotel separately, especially in late January and late April. Compare both before you book.

## Nearby airports that can save you money

- **Regina (YQR):** about two and a half hours south. Each city gets slightly different sun flights and sales, so it is worth comparing for specific trips.
- **Edmonton (YEG):** about five hours west, with more competition from Porter and Flair and more sun routes.
- **Calgary (YYC):** about six and a half hours away, with WestJet's long-haul flights to Europe and Asia.

US border airports are far from Saskatoon, so driving south rarely pays off. Our guide to [flying from US border airports]({{ '/guides/fly-from-us-border-airports/' | relative_url }}) explains how to run the numbers if you are already near the border.

## Getting to YXE without wasting money

The airport is in the city's north end, about a 10–15 minute drive from downtown, so taxis and rideshares are reasonable. On a budget, Saskatoon Transit Route 11 connects the airport with downtown, typically about once an hour. Check the current schedule before an early or late flight.

Parking at YXE is moderately priced; for a week-long sun trip, compare it with a return taxi.

## Tips for YXE

- Book sun flights for mid-January, late April or November.
- Compare Saskatoon and Regina for every sun trip.
- Grab good fares on single-carrier routes rather than waiting.
- For Europe, compare connections through Calgary, Toronto and, in summer, Halifax.
