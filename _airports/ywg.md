Winnipeg's best deals are winter sun escapes and domestic flights, and new WestJet routes plus growing Porter and Flair service give Manitobans more choice than a few years ago. The cheapest fares tend to appear in mid-January to February, late spring and the fall.

## Quick answer

- **Main carriers:** WestJet and Air Canada, with Porter and Flair adding competition.
- **Cheapest months:** mid-January to February, late April to May, and September to November.
- **Best-value routes:** Cancún, Punta Cana, Las Vegas, Phoenix, Toronto and Vancouver.
- **Cheap airport access:** Winnipeg Transit routes D12, D13 and 224.
- **Alternatives:** Grand Forks and Fargo for some US leisure flights.

## Airlines and where they fly

WestJet has been growing in Winnipeg, flying across Canada, to US sun spots and to a long list of winter sun destinations. For winter 2026–27 it announced new weekly nonstops to Punta Cana and Belize City, and in summer 2026 it added a weekly flight to Reykjavík, Winnipeg's first nonstop link to Iceland.

Air Canada connects Winnipeg with its hubs. Porter flies jets to Eastern Canada, including a newer route to Hamilton, while Flair offers low-cost domestic service. Calm Air and Perimeter Aviation connect the city with northern Manitoba and Nunavut, and US airlines link YWG with their hubs.

> **Heads up:** Winnipeg sits in the middle of the country, so it is roughly equidistant from both coasts. Domestic sales that cover all of Canada often make Winnipeg–Vancouver or Winnipeg–Toronto unusually good value.

## Cheapest times to fly from Winnipeg

Winter sun drives Winnipeg's fare calendar. Demand peaks over Christmas, New Year and spring break, then falls back in late January and again from April.

| Period | What to expect from YWG |
|---|---|
| Mid-January to February | Good sun fares outside Louis Riel Day weekend |
| Late March | Spring-break highs for Mexico and the Caribbean |
| Late April to May | Shoulder value to the US and within Canada |
| July–August | Peak summer pricing |

## Best-value routes from YWG

Cancún is Winnipeg's most reliable sun deal, with enough seats in winter that sales are common. Read our [Cancún guide]({{ '/destinations/cancun/' | relative_url }}) for typical fares. Puerto Vallarta, Punta Cana and Montego Bay are other favourites, and the new Belize City flight gives Manitobans a fresh Central American option.

Las Vegas and Phoenix are great short-notice escapes, especially for snowbirds who want to split up a long winter. Within Canada, Toronto, Vancouver, Calgary and Ottawa all see regular sales, helped by four airlines competing on some routes.

> **Pro tip:** New routes often launch with promotional fares. When an airline announces a new Winnipeg nonstop, check prices in the first few days, before the introductory seats sell out.

## Nearby airports that can save you money

Winnipeg is close enough to the US border that two North Dakota airports market themselves to Manitobans:

- **Grand Forks (GFK):** about two and a half hours south, with seasonal low-cost flights to sun spots. Service was paused and partly restored in 2026 as Canadian demand fell, so check current schedules.
- **Fargo (FAR):** about three and a half hours away, with more airlines and connections through US hubs.

Remember to factor in gas, parking, border waits, US dollar fares and the drive home after a late arrival. Our guide to [flying from US border airports]({{ '/guides/fly-from-us-border-airports/' | relative_url }}) walks through the full comparison.

## Getting to YWG without wasting money

The airport is close to the city centre, which keeps taxi and rideshare costs reasonable. On a budget, Winnipeg Transit serves the terminal with routes D12 Ellice, D13 Sargent and 224 Sherwin–Valour, introduced with the city's 2025 network overhaul. Buses run from early morning until after midnight, but check current times before an early flight.

Airport parking is moderately priced; for a week-long trip, compare it with a return taxi or transit.

## Tips for YWG

- Book sun trips for mid-January to February or for November.
- Compare package deals for Mexico and the Caribbean; Winnipeg often has good all-inclusive offers.
- Check whether a cheaper US border flight still makes sense once you add all costs.
- Look at one-stop itineraries through Calgary or Toronto for Europe.

Winnipeg is a strong airport for winter sun and domestic deals, with new WestJet routes and growing Porter and Flair service keeping prices honest.
