Victoria International is small and easy, and it has enough nonstop domestic, Seattle and winter sun flights to make it the right starting point for many Vancouver Island trips. The cheapest fares usually appear from mid-January to March, in late spring and in the fall, while long-haul travellers should weigh the ferry to Vancouver.

## Quick answer

- **Main carriers:** WestJet and Air Canada, with Porter, Alaska Airlines and Pacific Coastal adding options.
- **Cheapest months:** mid-January to March, late April to May, and October to November.
- **Best-value routes:** Vancouver, Calgary, Toronto, Puerto Vallarta, Cancún and Seattle.
- **Cheap airport access:** BC Transit routes serve the airport from Sidney, Saanich and, on some trips, downtown.
- **Alternative:** Vancouver via BC Ferries for long-haul and international trips.

## Airlines and where they fly

WestJet has the biggest schedule at YYJ, flying to Calgary, Vancouver, Edmonton, Toronto and other cities, plus seasonal sun routes to Mexico. Air Canada connects Victoria with its hubs in Vancouver, Calgary and Toronto, which is handy for international connections.

Porter flies Embraer jets east, including Victoria's longest nonstop to Ottawa, and Alaska Airlines links YYJ with Seattle for onward US connections. Pacific Coastal serves smaller BC communities. Floatplane and helicopter services to Vancouver leave from Victoria's Inner Harbour rather than the airport, and are quick but pricey.

> **Heads up:** US-bound flights from Victoria clear US preclearance before departure, so you land in Seattle as a domestic passenger. Allow extra time at busy periods.

## Cheapest times to fly from Victoria

Victoria is a popular summer destination, so July and August are expensive for everyone. Winter sun demand pushes up Mexico prices around Christmas and spring break, but domestic fares are often reasonable in winter.

| Period | What to expect from YYJ |
|---|---|
| Mid-January to March | Good domestic and sun fares outside spring break |
| Late April to May | Shoulder value before the summer rush |
| June to August | Peak prices in both directions |
| October to November | Often the best value of the year |

## Best-value routes from YYJ

Short domestic hops are Victoria's bread and butter. Calgary, Edmonton and Kelowna regularly go on sale, and Toronto and Ottawa see good fares when WestJet, Air Canada and Porter overlap.

For winter sun, WestJet's seasonal nonstops to Puerto Vallarta, Cancún and Los Cabos save islanders a connection in Vancouver. Book for late January, late April or November to avoid peak pricing. Our [Cancún guide]({{ '/destinations/cancun/' | relative_url }}) has a breakdown of typical fares.

For US trips, the Alaska Airlines link to Seattle is useful: one connection opens up California, Hawaii and much of the US.

> **Pro tip:** If you are heading to Los Angeles or Las Vegas, compare a Victoria–Seattle connection with a Victoria–Vancouver connection. The cheaper option changes often.

## Nearby airports that can save you money

- **Vancouver (YVR):** the big alternative. With BC Ferries from Swartz Bay to Tsawwassen plus transit, expect a long day, but YVR's nonstops to Asia, Europe and Hawaii can save enough to justify it.
- **Nanaimo (YCD):** about one and a half to two hours north, with a smaller domestic schedule. Worth checking if you live mid-island.
- **Seattle:** reachable by passenger ferry from Victoria's Inner Harbour or by a short Alaska flight, with a huge US domestic network.

## Getting to YYJ without wasting money

The airport is on the Saanich Peninsula near Sidney, about 25 km from downtown Victoria. BC Transit serves the terminal with routes such as 87 and 88, and from September 2026 some Route 72 trips have been linking downtown, the airport and Swartz Bay. Schedules have been changing, so confirm the current timetable before relying on the bus for an early flight.

Parking is convenient and generally cheaper than at YVR, which makes driving reasonable for shorter trips.

## Tips for YYJ

- Price long-haul trips from both Victoria and Vancouver, including ferry and transit costs.
- Book sun flights early in the season; nonstops from Victoria have fewer seats.
- Check whether a Seattle connection on Alaska is cheaper for US trips.
- Watch domestic sales from all three main carriers.

## Bottom line

Victoria is ideal for domestic, Seattle and winter sun trips, while Vancouver usually wins for Asia and Europe. Compare both, avoid summer peaks, and sign up for [free deal alerts]({{ '/deal-alerts/' | relative_url }}) to catch island-friendly fares.
