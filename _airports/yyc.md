Calgary is WestJet's home hub, which gives YYC an unusually big menu of nonstop winter sun, US and European flights for a city its size. Cheap fares cluster in late January–February, late spring and the fall, and Calgarians who are flexible on dates can often find excellent Mexico and Hawaii deals.

## Quick answer

- **Main carriers:** WestJet dominates, with Air Canada, Porter and Flair keeping prices in check.
- **Cheapest months:** late January to February, late April to May, and September to November.
- **Best-value routes:** Cancún, Puerto Vallarta, Las Vegas, Phoenix, Honolulu, Vancouver and London.
- **Cheap airport access:** Route 500 Airport Express to downtown or Route 100 to the CTrain Blue Line.
- **Alternatives:** Edmonton for some routes; US border airports are far and rarely worth it.

## Airlines and where they fly

WestJet runs its biggest operation in Calgary, with nonstop flights across Canada, to many US cities, and to a long list of seasonal sun destinations. Its Boeing 787s fly from Calgary to Europe and Asia, including daily service to Tokyo Narita, along with seasonal routes to cities such as London Gatwick, Paris, Dublin and Rome.

Air Canada connects Calgary to its other hubs and to London Heathrow, while Porter now runs daily jet service east and is adding US sun routes such as Phoenix for winter 2026–27. Flair adds low-cost competition on domestic and sun routes, including a returning Calgary–Cancún service this winter. US carriers link YYC with their hubs.

> **Heads up:** WestJet keeps adding winter sun routes from Calgary. For 2026–27 it announced a new nonstop to Mérida, Mexico, so check new routes each fall for launch deals.

## Cheapest times to fly from Calgary

Calgary's winter sun demand is intense, so sun fares peak at Christmas and during the February school break, then fall back in late January and again from April. Summer is busy for Europe and for domestic trips, and Stampede week pushes up prices to and from the city.

## Best-value routes from YYC

Winter sun is Calgary's specialty. Cancún, Puerto Vallarta, Los Cabos and Montego Bay all have nonstop service, and the sheer volume of seats means seat sales are frequent. See our [Cancún guide]({{ '/destinations/cancun/' | relative_url }}) for what to expect.

Hawaii is a close second; Honolulu and Maui fares soften after early January and again in spring. Las Vegas and Phoenix are short, often cheap hops that make great long weekends.

Europe and Asia are more affordable from Calgary than many travellers expect, because WestJet's widebody flights compete directly with Air Canada and British Airways. London is often the best-value transatlantic route, and Tokyo goes on sale for winter and fall travel.

> **Pro tip:** Domestic hops to Vancouver and Kelowna regularly drop to bargain levels when WestJet, Air Canada, Porter and Flair overlap. Set a fare watch rather than booking at the first price you see.

## Nearby airports that can save you money

Calgary has fewer practical alternatives than Toronto or Vancouver:

- **Edmonton (YEG):** about three hours north. Occasionally cheaper for routes where Flair or Porter run aggressive sales from Edmonton.
- **Montana airports (Kalispell or Great Falls):** five hours or more away, with limited service. Rarely worth the drive unless you are already heading south.

For most Calgarians, the better money-saver is flexibility on dates. Our guide to [flying from US border airports]({{ '/guides/fly-from-us-border-airports/' | relative_url }}) explains when the drive makes sense.

## Getting to YYC without wasting money

Calgary Transit has two main airport routes. Route 500 Airport Express, which replaced the old Route 300 name in fall 2026 with no change to routing, runs between the airport and downtown along Centre Street. Route 100 links the airport with Saddletowne station on the CTrain Blue Line and with the North Pointe terminal. A regular transit fare applies; check Calgary Transit for current times.

Airport parking is convenient but expensive for a week-long sun trip, so price out economy lots and off-site options.
