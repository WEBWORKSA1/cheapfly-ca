Hamilton's airport has been transformed by Porter, which started flying jets from YHM in June 2025 and has steadily added cities since. For travellers in Hamilton, Niagara, Halton and Brantford, the savings often come from cheaper parking, short lines and no drive to Pearson, with the best fares in January–February, late spring and the fall.

## Quick answer

- **Main carrier:** Porter, with Air Canada offering a coach connection to Pearson.
- **Cheapest months:** January to February, late April to May, and September to November.
- **Best-value routes:** Halifax, Vancouver, Calgary, Edmonton, Winnipeg and, from December 2026, Montego Bay.
- **Cheap airport access:** HSR Route 20 A-Line Express from downtown and the Hamilton GO Centre.
- **Alternatives:** Pearson for more choice, Buffalo for some US trips.

## Airlines and where they fly

Porter launched at Hamilton in June 2025 with jet flights to Vancouver, Halifax, Calgary and Edmonton, then added Winnipeg and St. John's in May 2026 and Ottawa from June 2026, with Montréal also announced. That doubled the airport's domestic destinations in roughly a year.

The next step is sun. Porter announced seasonal flights from Hamilton to Montego Bay, Jamaica, starting in December 2026. Air Canada does not fly from Hamilton, but it sells a coach connection, operated by The Landline Company, that links YHM with its flights at Pearson.

Hamilton has seen low-cost carriers come and go over the years. Iceland's PLAY ended its Hamilton flights in April 2025 before the airline shut down later that year, so check current schedules rather than older route lists.

> **Heads up:** The Landline coach to Pearson can only be booked as part of an Air Canada itinerary. Checked bags are transferred automatically on the way out, but on the way home you collect them at Pearson before boarding the coach.

## Cheapest times to fly from Hamilton

Hamilton follows the Ontario calendar: Christmas, March Break and summer are the busiest periods. Because Porter is the main airline, fares can rise sharply on popular dates, so booking early pays off.

| Period | What to expect from YHM |

| January–February | Good domestic fares outside Family Day weekend |
| March Break | Higher fares, especially for Montego Bay |
| Late April to May | Shoulder value to Western Canada |

## Best-value routes from YHM

Halifax and Western Canada are Hamilton's sweet spots. Porter's nonstops to Vancouver, Calgary and Edmonton save a trip to Pearson, and sales often bring fares in line with or below Toronto prices. Halifax and St. John's are popular for summer and fall visits east.

Montego Bay is the new sun option. Read our [Montego Bay guide]({{ '/destinations/montego-bay/' | relative_url }}) to see what a good fare and package price look like.

> **Pro tip:** Porter sometimes prices Hamilton and Pearson differently on the same route. Search both airports before you book; a short drive may save money in either direction.

## Nearby airports that can save you money

- **Toronto Pearson (YYZ):** about an hour away, with far more airlines and destinations, including Europe and the full sun network.
- **Billy Bishop (YTZ):** downtown Toronto, with Porter and Air Canada turboprops to Eastern Canada and the US.
- **Buffalo Niagara (BUF):** about an hour and a half from Hamilton, worth checking for US domestic trips.
- **Region of Waterloo (YKF):** about 45 minutes away, with a small schedule worth a quick check.

## Getting to YHM without wasting money

Parking is one of Hamilton's biggest advantages; long-term rates are generally well below Pearson's. On transit, HSR Route 20 A-Line Express links the airport with downtown Hamilton, Mohawk College's Fennell campus and the Hamilton GO Centre. From Toronto, GO Transit to Hamilton plus the A-Line is a budget option, though it takes time.

The airport upgraded its terminal in 2025, including its first passenger jet bridges.

## Tips for YHM

- Compare Hamilton and Pearson for every Porter route.
- Book Montego Bay early in the season, as new sun routes have limited seats.
- Count parking savings when comparing airports.
- Check whether your Porter fare includes a checked bag.
