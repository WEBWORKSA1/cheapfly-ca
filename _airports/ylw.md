Kelowna International is the Okanagan's main airport, and it has grown into a solid base for domestic, Seattle and winter sun flights, with a new departures hall opened in January 2026. The cheapest fares tend to appear from mid-January to February, in late spring and in the fall.

## Quick answer

- **Main carriers:** WestJet and Air Canada, with Porter, Alaska Airlines and Flair adding competition.
- **Cheapest months:** mid-January to February, late April to May, and October to November.
- **Best-value routes:** Vancouver, Calgary, Toronto, Cancún, Puerto Vallarta and Seattle.
- **Cheap airport access:** BC Transit Route 23 via the UBC Okanagan exchange.
- **Alternatives:** Penticton and Kamloops nearby, or Vancouver for long-haul trips.

## Airlines and where they fly

WestJet and Air Canada carry most passengers, linking Kelowna with Vancouver, Calgary, Edmonton and Toronto, with WestJet also flying seasonal sun routes. Porter flies Embraer jets to Toronto Pearson and added a second eastern route, to Ottawa, in 2026, while Flair offers low-cost domestic options.

Alaska Airlines is Kelowna's transborder workhorse, linking YLW with Seattle several times a day and, more recently, Los Angeles. Pacific Coastal serves smaller BC communities.

> **Heads up:** Kelowna is a destination as well as a departure point. Summer lake season and winter ski weekends bring visitors in, which pushes up fares for locals flying out on Fridays and back on Sundays.

## Cheapest times to fly from Kelowna

The Okanagan's tourism seasons shape YLW fares. Summer is peak, ski season adds weekend pressure from December to March, and Christmas and spring break bring sun-seeker demand.

| Period | What to expect from YLW |
|---|---|
| Mid-January to February | Good sun and domestic fares mid-week |
| March | Spring-break highs for Mexico |
| Late April to May | Shoulder value before summer |
| June to August | Peak prices, especially to and from Vancouver and Calgary |
| October to November | Often the best value of the year |

## Best-value routes from YLW

Short hops to Vancouver and Calgary are the most frequently discounted, with several airlines competing. Toronto is a good-value nonstop when WestJet, Air Canada and Porter overlap, especially in spring and fall.

For winter sun, Kelowna's seasonal nonstops to Mexico save a connection and a few hours each way. Cancún and Puerto Vallarta usually see the most seats, while Los Cabos and Mazatlán are smaller markets. See our [Cancún guide]({{ '/destinations/cancun/' | relative_url }}) for typical fares.

The Alaska link to Seattle is a strong option for US trips, and a Los Angeles nonstop makes California much easier from the Okanagan; see our [Los Angeles guide]({{ '/destinations/los-angeles/' | relative_url }}).

> **Pro tip:** For a Hawaii trip, compare a connection through Vancouver or Calgary with one through Seattle on Alaska. Prices vary a lot by season, and the cheapest routing changes often.

## Nearby airports that can save you money

- **Penticton (YYF):** about an hour south, with a small domestic schedule. Occasionally cheaper to Vancouver or Calgary.
- **Kamloops (YKA):** about two hours north, with flights to Vancouver and Calgary.
- **Vancouver (YVR):** about four hours away over the Coquihalla, worth it mainly for Asia, Europe or Hawaii.
- **Spokane (GEG), Washington:** roughly four and a half to five hours south through the border, with a decent US network. Factor in winter driving, the border and US dollar fares.

## Getting to YLW without wasting money

The airport is north of downtown Kelowna, near UBC Okanagan. BC Transit Route 23 serves the terminal and connects with the UBC Okanagan exchange, where RapidBus and other routes head into the city. Service is less frequent than on main city routes, particularly in evenings and on weekends, so check current times.

Parking at YLW is generally cheaper than at big-city airports, which makes driving sensible for short trips.

## Tips for YLW

- Fly mid-week to avoid ski and summer weekend demand.
- Book Mexico for late January, late April or November.
- Compare Alaska via Seattle against Canadian carriers for US trips.
- Price long-haul trips from Vancouver too, including the drive and parking.

## Bottom line

Kelowna is a convenient airport with growing competition, especially for domestic, Seattle and winter sun trips.
