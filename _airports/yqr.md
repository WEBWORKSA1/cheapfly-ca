Regina is a small, efficient airport whose best deals are WestJet winter sun nonstops and Air Canada and WestJet domestic sales. The cheapest fares usually appear from mid-January to February, in late spring and in the fall, and this winter brings a new nonstop to Los Cabos.

## Quick answer

- **Main carriers:** WestJet and Air Canada, with United linking Regina to Denver.
- **Cheapest months:** mid-January to February, late April to May, and September to November.
- **Best-value routes:** Cancún, Los Cabos, Phoenix, Calgary and Toronto.
- **Cheap airport access:** Regina Transit Route 24, weekdays only.
- **Alternative:** Saskatoon for different sun routes and sales.

## Airlines and where they fly

WestJet has the biggest schedule at YQR, with nonstop flights to Calgary, Edmonton, Vancouver, Winnipeg and Toronto, plus seasonal sun routes. For winter 2026–27 it announced a new nonstop from Regina to San José del Cabo, Mexico, alongside seasonal service to destinations such as Cancún, Puerto Vallarta and Phoenix.

Air Canada connects Regina with Toronto, Vancouver and Calgary for onward flights. For the US, United links YQR with Denver and WestJet has flown to Minneapolis–Saint Paul, which opens up American connections. Low-cost carriers have come and gone over the years; check whether Flair currently serves the routes you need.

> **Heads up:** Regina has fewer airlines than larger cities, so fares can jump close to departure. When you spot a fare near the deal threshold for your route, book it rather than waiting for a further drop.

## Best-value routes from YQR

Cancún is the most reliable winter sun deal from Regina, and the new Los Cabos nonstop adds a second Mexican option without a connection. Puerto Vallarta and Phoenix are popular for snowbirds, especially for longer stays in January and February.

Within Canada, Calgary, Vancouver and Toronto see regular sales. For Europe and Asia, you will usually connect in Calgary, Toronto or Montréal; compare those itineraries carefully, as connection times vary.

> **Pro tip:** If you are flying to Los Cabos for the first time, price the new nonstop early in the season. Launch-season fares are sometimes lower before demand settles.

## Getting to YQR without wasting money

Regina's airport is close to the city centre, so a taxi or rideshare is usually affordable. Regina Transit Route 24, launched in 2023 with support from the airport, runs between downtown and the terminal along 13th Avenue. It operates on weekdays only, with service concentrated in morning and evening periods, so check the timetable before relying on it.

Parking at YQR is reasonably priced compared with big-city airports, which makes driving practical for short trips.
