Toronto Pearson is Canada's busiest airport and usually its cheapest place to start an international trip, because almost every Canadian and foreign carrier competes here. The best deals tend to appear on sun routes, New York, Florida and Western Europe, especially in the January–February and fall shoulder seasons.

## Quick answer

- **Most competition in Canada:** Air Canada, WestJet, Porter, Flair and Air Transat all fly from YYZ, alongside dozens of foreign airlines.
- **Cheapest months:** mid-January to mid-February, late April to early June, and September to November.
- **Best-value routes:** Cancún, Punta Cana, New York, Orlando, London, Lisbon and Rome often dip well below typical prices.
- **Getting there cheaply:** UP Express from Union Station or the TTC's 900 Airport Express bus from Kipling station.
- **Check alternatives:** Billy Bishop for business-style trips, Hamilton for some domestic routes, and Buffalo for selected US flights.

## Airlines and where they fly

Pearson is Air Canada's largest hub, with nonstop flights across Canada, the US, Europe, Asia, Latin America and the Caribbean. WestJet runs a large domestic and sun operation here, and Porter moved its Embraer jets into Pearson, adding a third full-service competitor on routes to Western Canada, Florida, the US Southwest and the Caribbean.

Flair keeps pressure on prices for domestic and sun routes, while Air Transat focuses on leisure markets such as Portugal, Italy, France and the Caribbean. Foreign carriers add even more transatlantic competition, which is why Toronto is often Canada's cheapest gateway to Europe.

> **Heads up:** Pearson has two passenger terminals, 1 and 3. Check which one your airline uses before you leave home; the free Terminal Link train connects them.

## Cheapest times to fly from Toronto

Prices spike around Christmas, March Break and summer holidays, then fall away quickly.

## Best-value routes from YYZ

Sun destinations are where Toronto shines. Cancún, Punta Cana and Montego Bay have so many daily seats that fares regularly fall below typical levels, particularly for departures in May, early June and the fall. Note that Canadian service to Cuba was suspended in 2026 because of a jet fuel shortage on the island, so check the status of any Varadero or Cayo Coco trip before booking.

Transatlantic deals are another strength. London, Lisbon, Rome and Paris all see sales, and Air Transat's leisure-focused network often undercuts full-service carriers outside summer. For Asia, Tokyo nonstop flights tend to be cheapest from January to March and in November.

New York and Orlando see frequent seat sales; just watch for basic fares that exclude carry-on bags.

## Nearby airports that can save you money

The Toronto area has more airport choice than anywhere else in Canada:

- **Billy Bishop (YTZ):** a downtown airport with Porter and Air Canada turboprop flights to Eastern Canada and the US. Fares are not always cheaper, but you save on time and transit costs. See our [Billy Bishop guide]({{ '/airports/ytz/' | relative_url }}).
- **Hamilton (YHM):** Porter now flies to several Canadian cities from Hamilton, and parking is typically cheaper than at Pearson.
- **Buffalo Niagara (BUF):** worth comparing for US domestic trips, especially Florida, but factor in the drive, the border, US dollar fares and parking.

## Getting to YYZ without wasting money

The UP Express train links Union Station to Terminal 1 in about 25 minutes, with stops at Bloor and Weston. At the time of writing, Toronto Pearson lists a lower adult one-way fare with PRESTO than without it, so load a card or confirm the current fares before you travel.

On a tight budget, the TTC's 900 Airport Express bus runs from Kipling station on Line 2 for a regular TTC fare. GO Transit buses also link Pearson with surrounding regions. Pearson parking is expensive for long trips, so compare off-site lots or a return transit trip before you drive.

> **Pro tip:** If you are flying early in the morning, check the first UP Express departure time. For a dawn flight, a TTC overnight route or a shared ride can be cheaper than an airport hotel.

## Tips for YYZ

- Compare the same trip from Pearson and Billy Bishop on a [flight search]({{ '/tools/flight-search/' | relative_url }}); Porter and Air Canada fares differ by airport.
- For Europe, try open-jaw itineraries, such as into London and out of Paris, which sometimes price like a simple return.
- Watch for fall sun deals. Hurricane-season departures are often the cheapest of the year; buy travel insurance.

> **Example:** A traveller flexible on dates can often shave a meaningful amount off a Cancún trip simply by shifting departure from a Saturday to a Tuesday or Wednesday.

Pearson gives Toronto travellers the most choice and the most frequent sales in Canada. Fly in the shoulder seasons, compare YTZ and YHM for domestic trips, and set up [free deal alerts]({{ '/deal-alerts/' | relative_url }}) so you can pounce when a fare drops below the threshold for your route.
