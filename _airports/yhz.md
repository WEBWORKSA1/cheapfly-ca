Halifax Stanfield is Atlantic Canada's main gateway and, because it is one of the closest Canadian airports to Europe, it has become a surprisingly strong airport for cheap transatlantic flights. Look for the best fares in mid-January to February, May and the fall, with summer Europe sales usually launching in winter.

## Quick answer

- **Main carriers:** Air Canada and WestJet, with Porter, Flair and Air Transat adding competition.
- **Cheapest months:** mid-January to February, May, and late September to November.
- **Best-value routes:** London, Dublin, Lisbon, Paris, Toronto, Cancún and Orlando.
- **Cheap airport access:** Halifax Transit Route 320 regional express.
- **Alternatives:** Moncton and Charlottetown for some domestic and sun trips.

## Airlines and where they fly

WestJet has made Halifax its Atlantic gateway to Europe. In summer 2026 it flew nonstop from YHZ to nine European cities: Amsterdam, Barcelona, Dublin, Edinburgh, London Gatwick, Paris, and new routes to Lisbon, Madrid and Copenhagen. Reports suggest some of those may be trimmed for 2027, so treat the list as a guide, not a guarantee.

Air Canada flies to its hubs, to London Heathrow and, from June 2026, to Brussels. Porter links Halifax with Toronto, Ottawa, Montréal, Hamilton and other cities, and Flair adds low-cost domestic flights. Air Transat and WestJet run seasonal sun routes, and PAL Airlines serves regional Atlantic points.

> **Heads up:** Halifax's summer Europe flights are seasonal. Many run from roughly May to October, so off-season trips to Europe usually connect through Toronto or Montréal.

## Cheapest times to fly from Halifax

Halifax has two distinct markets: winter sun and summer Europe. Sun fares peak at Christmas and March Break, while Europe and domestic fares peak in July and August when visitors flood in.

## Best-value routes from YHZ

London is often Halifax's best transatlantic value, with Air Canada to Heathrow and WestJet to Gatwick competing for the same travellers. Flights are short, at roughly six hours, and sales for May, June and September can be excellent. Our [London guide]({{ '/destinations/london/' | relative_url }}) covers both airports.

Lisbon is the exciting newcomer. A nonstop from Halifax to Portugal opens up one of Europe's best-value cities without a connection in Toronto. Dublin and Edinburgh are also popular shoulder-season trips.

For winter, Cancún, Punta Cana and Orlando are the volume leaders. Within Canada, Toronto and Ottawa frequently go on sale thanks to Porter, Air Canada, WestJet and Flair overlapping.

> **Pro tip:** If you want to visit two European cities, price an open-jaw ticket, flying into one city and home from another. With several nonstops from Halifax, these can cost little more than a simple return.

## Nearby airports that can save you money

- **Moncton (YQM):** about two and a half hours away, with domestic flights including low-cost options to Toronto. Occasionally cheaper for Ontario trips.
- **Charlottetown (YYG):** a similar drive plus the Confederation Bridge toll on the way back; sometimes useful for domestic and sun flights.
- **Fredericton (YFC):** around four hours, with limited service.

US airports such as Bangor and Portland in Maine are a long drive away, so they rarely beat Halifax on total cost.

## Getting to YHZ without wasting money

Halifax Stanfield is about 35 km from downtown, so taxis are expensive. Halifax Transit's Route 320, listed as a regional express, connects the airport with downtown Halifax for a regular transit fare; the ride takes roughly an hour. Service is less frequent than city routes, so check the current timetable.

For longer trips, compare airport parking with transit or a shared ride.
