Billy Bishop Toronto City Airport is the downtown island airport, and its biggest saving is often time and transit costs rather than the fare itself. Porter and Air Canada fly turboprops to Eastern Canada and, since US preclearance opened in 2026, to more US cities; fares tend to be lowest on weekends, in summer and in the shoulder seasons.

## Quick answer

- **Main carriers:** Porter, which is based here, and Air Canada Express.
- **Cheapest times:** weekends and summer on business routes, plus January–February and October–November.
- **Best-value routes:** Montréal, Ottawa, New York, Boston, Chicago and Halifax.
- **Cheap airport access:** walk or take a free shuttle from the Union Station area.
- **Alternatives:** Pearson for jets and more destinations, Hamilton for some domestic routes.

## Airlines and where they fly

Porter is Billy Bishop's home airline, flying Dash 8-400 turboprops to cities across Ontario, Quebec and Atlantic Canada, including Ottawa, Montréal, Québec City and Halifax. Jets are not permitted at the island airport, so Porter flies its Embraer jets from Pearson instead.

Air Canada operates Air Canada Express flights to Montréal and Ottawa and expanded its schedule in 2026, including US routes. The big change this year was US preclearance, which opened in March 2026 and lets passengers clear US customs in Toronto before boarding.

From YTZ, Porter flies to US cities such as Boston, Chicago, Newark, Washington Dulles and, since spring 2026, Nashville. Porter's Chicago flights moved from Midway to O'Hare in September 2026. Air Canada added Boston, Chicago O'Hare, New York LaGuardia and Washington Dulles.

> **Heads up:** Because only turboprops fly here, range is limited. For Western Canada, Florida, the Caribbean or overseas, you will be flying from Pearson.

## Cheapest times to fly from Billy Bishop

Billy Bishop's main routes are business-heavy, which flips the usual pattern. Weekday mornings and Thursday or Friday afternoons are the most expensive, while Saturdays, Sunday mornings and summer holiday weeks can be cheaper.

## Best-value routes from YTZ

Montréal and Ottawa are the busiest routes, and with Porter and Air Canada both flying them frequently, sales are common. Leisure travellers who can fly mid-day or on weekends often get the best prices.

US cities are the growth story. New York is a classic weekend escape, and both airlines now compete on Boston, Chicago and Washington. > **Pro tip:** Porter fares include complimentary snacks and drinks, but its lowest fare types limit bags and changes. Compare like for like against Air Canada and against Pearson.

## Nearby airports that can save you money

- **Hamilton (YHM):** about an hour west, with Porter jet flights to several Canadian cities and cheaper parking.
- **Buffalo Niagara (BUF):** sometimes worth comparing for US trips, but factor in the drive, the border and US dollar fares.

## Getting to YTZ without wasting money

Getting to Billy Bishop is easy and cheap. You can walk through the pedestrian tunnel under the Western Gap or take one of the shortest ferry rides anywhere. A free shuttle bus has long linked the airport's mainland entrance with the Union Station area; check the airport's site for current details. TTC streetcars on Bathurst Street and Queens Quay stop close by, and cycling is a popular option.

Parking near the airport is limited and pricey, so transit or walking usually makes more sense.
