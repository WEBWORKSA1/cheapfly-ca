Vancouver is Canada's Pacific gateway, which makes YVR the best Canadian airport for cheap flights to Asia, Hawaii and the US West Coast. The lowest fares usually appear from January to March and in the fall, while summer is the priciest time to leave the city.

## Quick answer

- **Main carriers:** Air Canada and WestJet run hubs here, with Porter, Flair and Alaska Airlines adding domestic and transborder competition.
- **Cheapest months:** mid-January to March, late April to May, and October to early December.
- **Best-value routes:** Los Angeles, Las Vegas, Honolulu, Mexico, Tokyo and Toronto see the most frequent deals.
- **Cheap airport access:** the Canada Line SkyTrain from downtown in about 25 minutes.
- **Alternatives:** Abbotsford for domestic and sun flights, and Bellingham or Seattle for selected US trips.

## Airlines and where they fly

Air Canada uses Vancouver as its transpacific gateway, with nonstop flights to Japan, Korea, China, Australia and other Asia-Pacific points, plus a big domestic and US network. WestJet runs its western hub here, flying to Hawaii, Mexico, the US and across Canada.

Porter flies Embraer jets east to Toronto, Ottawa and Montréal, and Flair keeps domestic prices honest. Alaska Airlines connects YVR with Seattle and the US West Coast, while a long list of Asian carriers, including Japan Airlines, ANA, Cathay Pacific, Korean Air and EVA Air, compete on transpacific routes.

> **Heads up:** US-bound flights clear US preclearance at YVR before departure. Build in extra time at peak hours, especially in summer and around holidays.

## Cheapest times to fly from Vancouver

Vancouver's fare calendar is a little different from Eastern Canada's. Summer is peak season in both directions because so many visitors fly in, and winter sun demand pushes up Mexico and Hawaii prices around Christmas and spring break.

| Period | What to expect from YVR |
|---|---|
| Mid-January to March | Good Asia, Europe and US fares; Hawaii softens after holidays |
| Late April to May | Shoulder-season deals almost everywhere |
| June to August | Peak prices, especially to Europe and within Canada |
| October to early December | Strong Asia and Mexico deals before the holidays |

## Best-value routes from YVR

The US West Coast is Vancouver's bread and butter: Los Angeles, Las Vegas, San Francisco and Palm Springs regularly go on sale, often with short flight times under three hours. Hawaii is the other classic; Honolulu and Maui fares fall noticeably outside Christmas and spring break.

For Asia, Tokyo is the standout. With several nonstop options, Vancouver to Tokyo often prices well below what Eastern Canadians pay, especially for January–March and fall travel. Read our [Tokyo destination guide]({{ '/destinations/tokyo/' | relative_url }}) for what a good fare looks like.

Mexico is a strong winter option too. Puerto Vallarta, Cancún and Mexico City all have nonstop service, and Mexico City in particular is a good-value long weekend outside the holidays.

> **Pro tip:** For Europe, Vancouver nonstops can be pricey. A one-stop itinerary through Toronto, Montréal, Seattle or Reykjavík is often cheaper, so compare carefully on a [flight search]({{ '/tools/flight-search/' | relative_url }}).

## Nearby airports that can save you money

The Lower Mainland has real alternatives:

- **Abbotsford (YXX):** about an hour east, with low-cost and WestJet flights to Canadian cities and some sun spots. Parking is cheaper and lines are shorter.
- **Bellingham (BLI), Washington:** a small US airport about an hour from Vancouver without border delays. Allegiant and Alaska serve it, though Canadian traffic has fallen and schedules have shrunk; check what is still flying.
- **Seattle (SEA):** a huge network with many low fares on US domestic routes, but you will pay in time, border waits and US dollars.

Our guide to [flying from US border airports]({{ '/guides/fly-from-us-border-airports/' | relative_url }}) walks through the true cost comparison.

## Getting to YVR without wasting money

The Canada Line SkyTrain links downtown Vancouver's Waterfront station to YVR–Airport station in about 25 minutes, with frequent service through the day. Heading into town, an extra airport surcharge (the YVR AddFare) applies to trips that start at the airport stations; check TransLink's current fare before you tap.

Driving is easy, but airport parking adds up on longer trips. Many travellers leave the car at a Canada Line station park-and-ride or use off-site lots.

> **Example:** Two travellers heading to Maui for a week may find that a round trip on the Canada Line costs far less than a week of airport parking.

## Tips for YVR

- Book Hawaii and Mexico for the second half of January or late April, when winter demand eases.
- For Asia, watch for fall sales; Tokyo often drops for November and early December travel.
- If you live in the Fraser Valley, always run the same search from Abbotsford.
